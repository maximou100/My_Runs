# My Runs

A personal iOS running tracker. All your runs, in one place.

My Runs imports your full running history — from Nike Run Club exports, TCX files, or Apple Health — and turns it into a fast, beautiful, private dashboard for iPhone. Everything stays on your device or in your own iCloud account.
